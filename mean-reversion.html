Mean Reversion in Seasonal Commodities

Data Analytics · Python · Live research

Learning to algorithmically backtest technical momentum strategies, it seemed easy to conclude that they alone possess insufficient data for speculation. This led me to wonder whether inverting this behaviour and applying it to a historically or structurally range-bound seasonal asset might outperform buy and hold. This descriptive analysis tests whether a theoretical edge exists and whether its performance can be attributed to seasonality.

Overview
  • Question: Does a mean reversion strategy beat buy-and-hold on seasonal commodity ETFs, and do more seasonal periods perform better?
  • Strategy averaged +48% per 3-year window against −7% for buy-and-hold. It beat buy-and-hold in 14 of 20 windows.
  • Disclaimer: This a binary exposure backtest purley for educational purposes
